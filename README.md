# Valley Tracker

A simple, phone-friendly tracker for Disney Dreamlight Valley.

- **Daily activities:** name, notes, and a "Mark complete" toggle that clears at the daily reset time.
- **Villagers:** friendship level, specialty, notes, today's gift, and a daily gift toggle.
- **Favorites:** tap the star on any card to keep it at the top of its section.
- **Sorting:** not gifted first, A–Z, or level high-to-low / low-to-high.
- **Themes:** purple, blue, or pink (Settings & backup → Color theme).

Everything is saved in your browser on that device. Use **Settings & backup → Copy backup** to keep a copy, and **Restore from backup** to load it on another device.

The whole site is the single file `index.html`.
